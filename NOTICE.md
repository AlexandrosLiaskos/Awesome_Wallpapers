## The wallpapers

The images in `images/` are **not** covered by the MIT licence.

- They were collected from [D3Ext/aesthetic-wallpapers](https://github.com/D3Ext/aesthetic-wallpapers), unless an entry in [`data/wallpapers.yml`](data/wallpapers.yml) names another source.
- Each one belongs to the artist or rights holder who made it. This project claims no ownership of any of them.
- Where the artist is known, the entry carries a `credit`, and the wallpaper's page shows it. Most artists are not known to us. If you recognise a piece, tell us and we will add the credit.
- Names, logos and characters that appear in some wallpapers are trademarks of their respective owners.

They are shared here for personal use as desktop and phone backgrounds, as they were in the collection they come from. This project cannot grant rights to them; for any other use, ask the artist.

## Credit and removal

If you are the artist or rights holder of a wallpaper here and would like it credited or removed, [open an issue](https://github.com/AlexandrosLiaskos/Awesome_Wallpapers/issues/new/choose) and say which one. No proof beyond a reasonable explanation is asked for, and a removal takes the file out of both the repository and the gallery.
